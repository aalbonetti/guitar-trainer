### Drone voicings
Open strings ringing against moving fretted notes: the shimmering, big-room sound of modern worship electric. The trick is chords where the **top two open strings (B and E)** belong to every chord in the key.

### Key of E
{{chords:E,Asus2,Bsus4_drone,Csharpm7_drone}}

Open B and E ring through **E – Asus2 – Bsus4 – C#m7** (1, 4, 5, 6m). Only the lower fingers move.

### Key of D
{{chords:D,Dsus2,A7sus4,G_over_B,Em7}}

In D, the open D and top strings tie the chords together.

### Practice
1. Key of E: one bar each, E – B(sus4) – C#m7 – A(sus2), the 1–5–6m–4 shape. Let everything ring.
2. Add a little drive and a long reverb. These voicings were made for it.
3. Try the key of D set: **D – G/B – Em7 – A7sus4** (1 – 4 – 2m – 5), letting the open D and top strings ring.
