### Dynamics make the song
A worship song breathes: quiet verses, building pre-choruses, big choruses, a bridge that climbs, a quiet tag. Your strumming is the main volume knob on acoustic.

### Three levels
**Quiet (verse 1, tag)**: one strum per bar, let it ring.

{{rhythm:S9_whole}}

**Medium (verse 2, pre-chorus)**: half notes, or soft eighths.

{{rhythm:S10_half}}

**Full (chorus)**: the worship pattern, or eighths with accents.

### The percussive chunk
Slap the strings with the side of your strumming hand on 2 and 4 instead of strumming. It's a built-in snare drum, great when there's no drummer.

{{rhythm:S6_chunk}}

1. Strum down on 1, then let your palm fall flat across the strings on 2: a dull "chk".
2. Keep the eighth-note motion: D U X U D U X U.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=76,beats=4,sub=2}}

> Build gradually. Going from whole notes straight to full strumming sounds like a gear change; go through half notes or soft eighths on the way up.

### Practice
Play one song's verse, pre-chorus and chorus with the three levels. Record it on your phone and listen: can you hear the build?

### Check yourself
When you can move between the levels on purpose, on two different days, mark it done.
