### Barre chords unlock the neck
One finger flat across all six strings (the **barre**) acts as a movable nut. Learn two shapes and you can play any major or minor chord anywhere.

### F (E-shape barre)
{{chord:F}}

The E major shape, played by fingers 2-3-4, with the index barring fret 1. Move it up: fret 3 is G, fret 5 is A, fret 7 is B. The name comes from the note under your index on **string 6** (F.07).

{{shape:E_shape_major@1}}

### Bm (A-shape minor barre)
{{chord:Bm}}

The Am shape with the index barring from string 5. Root on **string 5**: fret 2 is B, fret 3 is C, fret 5 is D.

{{shape:A_shape_minor@2}}

### Making it ring
- Roll the index finger slightly onto its **bony edge** (toward the headstock).
- Pull back gently with the **arm**, don't squeeze with the thumb.
- Check each string one at a time. A dead string means a knuckle crease is under it: shift the barre slightly up or down.
- Hold the shape 10 seconds, release, shake out, repeat. Short sessions, every day. Strength comes in weeks, not hours.

> If your hand hurts (not just the fingertips), stop. Pain in the wrist or thumb joint means too much squeezing.

### Use it
Replace Fmaj7 with F in **C – Am – F – G**, and the partial Bm with a full Bm in **D – Bm – G – A**.

### Goal
80% of strums ring clean on F and Bm.
