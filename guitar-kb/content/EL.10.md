### Write your own hooks
Many songs' recorded guitar hooks are copyrighted, so it's better (and more fun) to write your own. A good hook is short, singable and repeated.

### The recipe: motif, repeat, vary
1. **Motif**: 3–5 notes from the major pentatonic, with a clear rhythm. Start or end on the root or 3rd.
2. **Repeat** it exactly. Repetition makes it a hook.
3. **Vary** the third time: change the last note to fit the chord, or move it up an octave.
4. **Resolve** on the fourth: end on the song's 1.

### Make it worship-ready
- Play it in **octaves** (EL.04) for intros, as single notes with **dotted-eighth delay** (EL.08) for turnarounds.
- Put it in the **intro**, after each chorus (turnaround), and in the **tag** (ER.08).
- Fit it to 1–5–6m–4: each repetition starts on a new chord.

### Practice
Write one hook for any song in Songs. Record it on your phone. Play it the next day: still good? Keep it.
