### Hearing where the song goes
Most worship songs move between **1, 4, 5 and 6m**. If you can hear which one comes next, you can follow a song you've never played, and recover when you get lost.

### What each move sounds like
- **1 → 4**: a lift, like opening a door. Feels like the song is "going somewhere" but relaxed.
- **1 → 5**: tension, a question that wants to come back home to 1.
- **1 → 6m**: the floor drops a little; same notes, darker mood. Common at the start of choruses and bridges.

### Practice
1. In G, play **G → C**, **G → D**, **G → Em**, a few times each, and name the move out loud.
2. Then take the quiz: it plays chord 1 and then the next chord, in random keys.
3. Listen for the **bass note**: it moves up 5 frets for the 4, up 7 for the 5, and up 9 (or down 3) for the 6m.

80% on the ear quiz.
