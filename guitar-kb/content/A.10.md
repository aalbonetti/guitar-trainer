### Fingers instead of a pick
Arpeggios (one string at a time) make intros and quiet verses beautiful. The thumb (**p**) plays the bass note; index, middle and ring (**i m a**) play strings 3, 2 and 1.

### PIMA
{{rhythm:P2_pima}}

- Thumb on the chord's **root**: string 6 for G and Em, string 5 for C and Am, string 4 for D.
- Index stays on string 3, middle on 2, ring on 1. Each finger owns a string.

### Rolling (3/4)
{{rhythm:P3_rolling_34}}

p i m a m i fits a 3/4 bar perfectly. Try it on Amazing Grace in A.11.

### Practice
1. Hold C. Play p-i-m-a slowly until it's even. Every note the same volume.
2. Change chords each bar: **C – Am – Fmaj7 – G**. Move the thumb to each chord's root.
3. Add the click at 60, then 70.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=70,beats=4,sub=2}}

> No fingernails? Fine. The pad of the finger gives a softer, warmer tone that suits quiet verses.

### Goal
4 bars of even PIMA at 70 BPM eighths.
