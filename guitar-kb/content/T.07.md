### Beats and subdivisions
**BPM** = beats per minute. Each beat can be split:

| Subdivision | Notes per beat | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter notes | 1 | 1 2 3 4 |
| Eighth notes | 2 | 1 & 2 & |
| Triplets | 3 | 1 trip let |
| Sixteenth notes | 4 | 1 e & a |

One beat lasts **60000 ÷ BPM** milliseconds. At 120 BPM a beat is 500 ms; at 72 BPM it's 833 ms.

### The dotted-eighth delay
The signature worship electric sound: you pick steady **eighth notes**, and a delay set to a **dotted eighth** (¾ of a beat) fills in the gaps so it sounds like sixteenths.

**Dotted-eighth delay (ms) = 60000 ÷ BPM × 0.75**

| BPM | 64 | 72 | 80 | 100 | 120 | 136 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delay (ms) | 703 | 625 | 563 | 450 | 375 | 331 |

Starting settings: 2–4 repeats (feedback around 30%), mix just under your dry signal. On the Kemper or a pedal, tap tempo usually has a dotted-eighth division; use that if you have it.

### Goal
90% on the delay-time quiz. Work it out in your head (or on the phone's calculator); within a few ms is close enough to choose the right answer.
