### Map the song before you play it
Great worship electric parts come from planning **what** to play in **each section**, not from playing more. Here's a typical dynamics map:

| Section | Energy | Electric part |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 2–4 / 10 | Hook in octaves, or swells |
| Verse 1 | 2–3 | Swells, pads, or nothing |
| Pre-chorus | 4–5 | Root pulse with dotted-eighth delay |
| Chorus | 7–8 | Triads or drone voicings, light drive |
| Verse 2 | 4–5 | Sparse triads or pulse |
| Bridge | builds 3 → 9 | Pulse → full chords, more drive |
| Final chorus | 9–10 | Biggest part: octaves or full chords |
| Tag / outro | 3 | Swells, harmonics, the hook again |

### Tone follows the map
Clean with long reverb for verses; edge of breakup with delay for pre-choruses; light overdrive in choruses; more drive for the bridge peak. Set up presets (on the Kemper, a rig per section or a performance) so changes are one tap.

### Practice
1. Pick a song from this month. Write its sections down.
2. Assign one part from ER.01–ER.07 to each section.
3. Play through with the click, changing parts **exactly** at the section change.
