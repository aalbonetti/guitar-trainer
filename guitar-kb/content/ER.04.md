### Triads: the electric player's chords
A triad is just the three notes of a chord (root, 3rd, 5th). On the top three strings they're small, bright and cut through the band without clashing with the acoustic. Learn three shapes per chord, the **inversions**, and you can play any chord in three places.

### Move them
Every shape is movable. Slide the whole shape up 2 frets for A, 5 for C, 7 for D. The minor version of each shape is one note lower (the 3rd drops a fret).

### Voice leading: stay close
When the chord changes, pick the inversion of the next chord that's **nearest** to where your hand already is. The notes barely move, and it sounds smooth and professional.
