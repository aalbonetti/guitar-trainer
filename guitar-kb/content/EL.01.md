### Why major pentatonic
Worship songs are mostly in **major keys**, and the **major pentatonic** (5 notes: 1 2 3 5 6) is almost impossible to make sound wrong over them. In G that's **G A B D E**.

### The main box (root on string 6, fret 3)
{{scale:G_major_pent_pos2}}

- Orange dots are the root, **G**. Start and end your ideas there.
- Fingers: index covers fret 2, middle fret 3, ring fret 4, pinky fret 5.
- **Movable**: put the root on string 6 at the song's key: A = fret 5, C = fret 8, D = fret 10, E = fret 12 (or 0).

### Practice
1. Up and down the box in eighth notes, alternate picking, 60 BPM. Say the root out loud each time you hit it.
2. Three-note groups: G A B, A B D, B D E… up the box, then down.
3. Make a melody: pick 3 notes, play them in a rhythm, repeat it twice, change the last note. That's a worship lead line.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=80,beats=4,sub=2}}

> Lead playing is singing with your hands. Fewer notes, held longer, beats fast runs every time in worship.

### Goal
4 bars of the box in eighths at 80 BPM, clean and even.
