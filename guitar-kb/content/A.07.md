### Not everything is in 4
Many hymns and some modern songs count in **3** (waltz) or in **6/8** (two big beats, each split in three).

### 3/4 waltz
Count **1** 2 3, **1** 2 3. Bass-heavy down on 1, lighter strums on 2 and 3:

### 6/8 feel
Six eighth notes per bar, felt as **two** beats: **1** 2 3 **4** 5 6. Accent 1 and 4.

{{rhythm:S5_six_eight}}

{{tool:metronome:bpm=60,beats=2,sub=3,meter=6/8}}

> A 6/8 song's BPM is usually counted on the big beat (the dotted quarter), so 60 BPM in 6/8 has six eighths per 2 clicks. The metronome above is set up that way.

### Practice plan
1. Waltz on G – C – D – G, one bar each, 90 BPM.
2. 6/8 on Em – C – G – D.
3. Play **Amazing Grace** (3/4) in A.11 with this strum.

### Goal
8 bars of 3/4 at 90 BPM.
