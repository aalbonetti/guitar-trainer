### The root pulse
The go-to electric part for pre-choruses and early bridges: **eighth notes on the chord's root**, high on the neck, with a **dotted-eighth delay** filling in between.

### Set the delay
- Delay time = 60000 ÷ BPM × 0.75 (T.07). At 100 BPM: **450 ms**.
- Feedback around 30% (2–4 repeats), mix just under your dry sound.
- On a Kemper or most delay pedals, set the tap division to "dotted eighth" and tap the song's tempo.

{{tool:delay}}

### Play it
{{exercise:EX_root_pulse_G}}

- Pick even, light eighths. Light palm mute optional.
- The delay answers each note a dotted eighth later, so your eighths turn into a galloping sixteenth pattern. If it sounds messy, your timing is off or the delay time is wrong.
- Change notes **exactly** on the chord change.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=100,beats=4,sub=2}}

> Without the delay pedal set up, practice it dry first. The timing has to be perfect on its own.

### Goal
16 bars at 100 BPM, the pulse locked to the click.
