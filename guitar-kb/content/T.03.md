### Numbers instead of letters
In the Nashville Number System each chord is written as its **scale degree** in the song's key. In G, **1** is G, **4** is C, **5** is D, **6m** is Em. The same chart works in every key, which is why worship teams use it: the leader can change key and nobody needs a new chart.

| Number | 1 | 2m | 3m | 4 | 5 | 6m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key of G | G | Am | Bm | C | D | Em |
| Key of D | D | Em | F#m | G | A | Bm |
| Key of A | A | Bm | C#m | D | E | F#m |
| Key of E | E | F#m | G#m | A | B | C#m |
| Key of C | C | Dm | Em | F | G | Am |

### Reading the marks
- **5/7** = the 5 chord with the 7th degree in the bass (in G: **D/F#**). Slash means "over".
- **4(2)** or **4²** = 4 chord with an added 2 (in G: **Cadd9**).
- A diamond or underline = hold it. **^** = push (play it an eighth early).
- Bars with several numbers split the bar evenly.

### Try it on a real song
Open a song and tap **Chords** to flip to **Numbers**. Change the key: the numbers stay the same.
