### Two notes at once
**Double stops** (two notes played together) sound sweeter than single notes and lighter than chords: a classic worship and country sound under a vocal or in a turnaround.

### 3rds (strings 3 and 2)
- Same fret on strings 3 and 2 = a **major 3rd**.
- String 2 one fret lower than string 3 = a **minor 3rd**.

### 3rds (strings 2 and 1)
- String 1 one fret lower than string 2 = a **major 3rd**.
- String 1 two frets lower = a **minor 3rd**.

### 6ths (strings 3 and 1, skip string 2)
- Same fret = a **major 6th**.
- String 1 one fret lower = a **minor 6th**.
- Mute string 2 with the underside of the finger on string 3, or pluck with pick and middle finger.

### In the key of G
Walk up the G major scale in 6ths on strings 3 and 1. Each pair is either "same fret" or "string 1 one fret lower", depending on where it falls in the scale: G/E, A/F# and C/A, D/B are same-fret; B/G, E/C and F#/D are one fret lower. Slide between them.

### Practice
Over a slow G – C – D loop, slide a 6th shape into a chord tone on each change.
