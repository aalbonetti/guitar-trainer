### The full major scale
Pentatonic is safe; the **major scale** adds the two "color" notes (4 and 7) for melodies and fills.

{{scale:G_major_pos2}}

Middle finger on string 6, fret 3 (G). One finger per fret, with stretches on fret 2. Movable: A at fret 5, C at fret 8, D at fret 10.

### Chord-tone targeting
The secret of melodic lead lines: on every chord change, **land on a note from the new chord** (its root, 3rd or 5th).

| Chord (in G) | Chord tones |
|---|---|
| G (1) | G B D |
| C (4) | C E G |
| D (5) | D F# A |
| Em (6m) | E G B |

Notice **B → C** and **F# → G**: the half-step moves between chords are what make lines sound "composed".

2. Then land on the **3rd** of each chord (B, E, F#, B): it's the most singable target.
