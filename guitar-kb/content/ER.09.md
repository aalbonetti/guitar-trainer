### Big choruses need big chords
For the loudest moments (final chorus, bridge peak) full barre chords with drive give the wall-of-sound. Four shapes cover nearly everything.

### 1–5–6m–4 in G with barres
- **G**: E-shape at fret 3
- **D**: A-shape at fret 5
- **Em**: A-shape minor at fret 7 (root E on string 5), or E-shape minor at fret 12
- **C**: A-shape at fret 3

### With drive
- Use less gain than you think: more gain = more mud.
- Palm mute in the build, open up for the chorus.
- Fret-hand mute between chords so changes are silent, not scratchy.
