### The same notes, a different home
Every major key has a **relative minor** on its 6th degree (T.02). G major and **E minor** share the same notes; A minor shares C major's. The minor pentatonic box is the most famous guitar shape there is.

### A minor pentatonic, position 1
{{scale:Am_pent_pos1}}

Notes A C D E G. This exact box is also **C major pentatonic**: same frets, just treat C as home.

### Connect the boxes
{{scale:Am_pent_pos2}}

{{scale:Am_pent_pos5}}

- **Position 2** sits just above position 1 and overlaps it. Slide between them on string 3 or 2.
- **Position 5** is position 1's neighbor below (it's the open-position shape, an octave up).
- Moving between boxes is how you climb the neck during a solo.

### Use it in a major key
Over a song in **C**, play the A minor boxes and target **C**. Over a song in **G**, slide everything down 2 frets (E minor pentatonic) and target **G**.

### Practice
1. Each box up and down at 80 BPM eighths.
2. Climb: a phrase in position 5, slide up into 1, finish in 2.
