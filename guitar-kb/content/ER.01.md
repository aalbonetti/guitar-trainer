### Electric is a different instrument
The same chord shapes sound completely different through an amp. Electric worship guitar is about **tone, control and space** more than strumming everything.

### Start clean
- Set a **clean** sound: no drive, a little reverb. On a Kemper, a clean profile with the gain low. On a pedalboard, everything off except reverb.
- Guitar volume on full, tone around 7–8 of 10. Neck or middle pickup for warmth, bridge for bite.
- Roll back some **low end** on the amp: the bass player and acoustic cover it.

### Pick attack
Electric strings are lighter and the amp exaggerates everything. Pick softer than on acoustic, and listen: a lighter attack sounds fuller and more even.

### Muting: the most important electric skill
On electric, every string you don't mute will ring and build noise. Two tools:

1. **Fret-hand muting**: let the underside of your fretting fingers lightly touch the strings you're not playing. Releasing pressure on a fretted note stops it instantly.
2. **Palm muting**: rest the side of your picking hand on the strings right where they meet the bridge saddles. Too far forward = dead thud; on the saddles = tight "chug" that still has pitch.

### Practice
1. Play a single note on string 4 fret 5. Mute every other string so nothing else rings, even if you strum all six.
2. Palm mute that note at 80 BPM eighths. Then lift the palm on beat 1 of each bar for an accent.

### Check yourself
