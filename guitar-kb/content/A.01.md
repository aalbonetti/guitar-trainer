### Your first two chords
E minor and G are in hundreds of worship songs, and they share fingers, which makes the change friendly.

{{chords:Em,G,G_4f}}

### E minor
Middle finger on string 5, fret 2; ring finger on string 4, fret 2. Strum all six strings. That's it.

### G (two ways)
- **3-finger G** (`320003`): middle on string 6 fret 3, index on string 5 fret 2, ring on string 1 fret 3.
- **4-finger G** (`320033`): middle on string 6 fret 3, index on string 5 fret 2, **ring on string 2 fret 3, pinky on string 1 fret 3**. Learn this one too: it's the anchor for the whole G family in the next lesson.

### The change
The middle finger barely moves: from string 5 fret 2 (Em) to string 6 fret 3 (G), one string over and one fret up. Lead with it, then drop the index onto string 5 fret 2 and the ring (and pinky) onto fret 3 of the top strings.

1. Place Em. Lift all fingers 1 cm. Place G. Repeat 10 times **without strumming**, just training the shapes.
2. Now strum: 4 downstrums of Em, 4 of G, with the click at 60 BPM.
3. **One-minute changes:** count how many clean Em ↔ G switches you make in 60 seconds. Write it down.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=60,beats=4,sub=1}}

> Don't wait for every finger to land before you strum. Keep the strumming hand moving; the fingers catch up within a week.

### Goal
30 clean changes a minute.
