### The click is the band
Every worship team plays to a click or a drummer, and the song falls apart if one player drifts. Learning to sit *exactly* on the click is the most useful skill you'll build.

### Count-in
The leader or click gives one or two bars before the song starts: "**1, 2, 3, 4**". Feel the beat in your body (tap a foot) before you play.

### Listen for the "disappearing click"
When you're perfectly in time, your strum covers the click and it seems to vanish. If you hear the click *before* your strum, you're late. If you hear it *after*, you're rushing.

> Count out loud: "one, two, three, four". It feels silly, and it works. Your mouth won't drift the way your hand does.

### Practice plan
1. 8 bars at 60 BPM, counting out loud.
2. 8 bars at 70 BPM.
3. Try the first beat of each bar a little louder (accent), the way a drummer hits the kick.

### Goal
8 bars of downstrums at 70 BPM, landing on the click.
