### Why the spider
It's a warm-up that trains every finger to press its own fret, cleanly, in time. Two minutes at the start of practice pays off in every lesson after.

### How
- One finger per fret: index on fret 1, middle on 2, ring on 3, pinky on 4.
- Play frets 1-2-3-4 on string 6, then string 5, and so on up to string 1.
- **Leave each finger down** until you need it on the next string.
- Pick strictly alternating: down, up, down, up.
- When that feels easy, come back down from string 1 to string 6 (4-3-2-1), then shift the whole thing up one fret.

### Tempo plan
Start slow enough that every note is clean, then let the app push you up the tempo ladder:
1. You start at **39 BPM** playing eighth notes (two notes per click). That's two-thirds of the goal.
2. After 3 clean runs in a row, the app adds 4 BPM. A really tight run jumps 8 at once.
3. If two runs in a row get sloppy, it drops back 8.

The button below always clicks at your current ladder tempo.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=ladder,beats=4,sub=2}}

> Clean beats fast. If a note buzzes or dies, the tempo is too high.

### Goal
The pattern at 60 BPM eighths, every note clean and on the click.
