### Eighth notes
Now the strumming hand moves **down on the numbers and up on the "&"s**, like a pendulum that never stops:

{{rhythm:S2_eighths}}

- Down strums hit mostly the lower strings, up strums mostly the higher ones. Don't try to hit all six on the way up.
- The motion comes from the **wrist and forearm**, loose, like shaking water off your hand.

### Accents on 2 and 4
Drummers hit the snare on beats 2 and 4. Strum those two beats a little harder and the guitar suddenly "grooves" with the band:

**1** & **2** & **3** & **4** & → soft, soft, **LOUD**, soft, soft, soft, **LOUD**, soft.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=70,beats=4,sub=2}}

### Practice plan
1. On one chord (G), straight eighths at 60 BPM, no accents, 8 bars.
2. Same, with accents on 2 and 4.
3. Change chords every bar: G – Cadd9 – Em7 – Dsus4. Keep the hand moving through the change, even if the first up-strum after the change catches open strings. That's normal and sounds fine.
4. Push to 80 BPM.

### Goal
8 bars of eighth notes at 80 BPM, average error ≤ 40 ms.
