### Play in any key with five shapes
With a capo, the G, C, D, E and A shapes you know can play a song in **any** key. Most worship acoustic players live here.

| Song key | Easiest options |
|---|---|
| A | capo 2 · G shapes |
| Bb | capo 3 · G shapes, or capo 1 · A shapes |
| B | capo 4 · G shapes, or capo 2 · A shapes |
| Db | capo 1 · C shapes |
| Eb | capo 1 · D shapes |
| F | capo 1 · E shapes, or capo 3 · D shapes |
| Ab | capo 1 · G shapes, or capo 4 · E shapes |

### How to choose
- Pick the shape key with the **lowest capo** that still sounds good.
- G shapes give the full, open worship sound (and the G family anchor). D shapes ring bright. E shapes are big and low.
- If another acoustic is playing, choose different shapes higher up the neck so you don't double each other.

### In the app
Open any song, set the **Key** to the leader's key, then use **Capo** to pick shapes. The chord line and diagrams switch to what your hands play.

### Practice
1. Open three songs from this month. For each, choose a capo and shapes, and play through the chorus.
2. Check yourself with the capo quiz from T.04.
3. Pass it: the app names a key and the capo + shapes for it; put the capo on and play 1–5–6m–4 twice, four strums per chord. It checks the chords as they sound with the capo, so a missing capo shows up.
