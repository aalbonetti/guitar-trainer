### The major scale
Every major scale uses the same pattern of whole (W) and half (H) steps:

**W W H W W W H**

Starting on G: G (W) A (W) B (H) C (W) D (W) E (W) F# (H) G. The notes are numbered **1 to 7**.

### Chords from the scale
Stack every other note of the scale on each degree and you get seven chords. The pattern is the same in every key:

| Degree | I | ii | iii | IV | V | vi | vii° |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality | major | minor | minor | major | major | minor | diminished |
| In G | G | Am | Bm | C | D | Em | F#° |
| In D | D | Em | F#m | G | A | Bm | C#° |
| In C | C | Dm | Em | F | G | Am | B° |

Almost every worship song lives on **I, IV, V and vi**, with ii and iii as color. Learn the pattern, not the letters, and any key becomes easy.

> The **vi** chord is the relative minor: G major and E minor use exactly the same notes.
