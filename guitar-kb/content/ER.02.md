### Power chords
Just the root and the 5th (and often the octave). No 3rd, so they're neither major nor minor, which is why they work everywhere and stay clean with drive.

{{shape:power_r6@3}}

{{shape:power_r5@3}}

- **Root on string 6**: index on the root, ring and pinky two frets up on strings 5 and 4. Fret 3 = G5, fret 5 = A5.
- **Root on string 5**: same shape one string over. Fret 3 = C5, fret 5 = D5.
- Mute the strings you're not playing with the underside of the index finger.

Use F.07 to name the root: the note under your index finger is the chord name.

### Practice: 1–5–6m–4 in G
**G5 (string 6, fret 3) → D5 (string 5, fret 5) → E5 (string 6, fret 0 or 12) → C5 (string 5, fret 3)**, one bar each, chugging eighths.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=90,beats=4,sub=2}}

> Downstrokes only, all the way. It's tiring at first; keep the wrist loose and the motion small.

### Goal
8 bars of eighth-note chug at 90 BPM, average error ≤ 40 ms.
