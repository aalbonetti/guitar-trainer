### Swells and pads
Remove the pick attack and a guitar sounds like strings or a synth pad. Great for intros, verse 1 and tags.

### Volume swells
1. Turn the guitar's volume knob (or the volume pedal) **all the way down**.
2. Pick a note or chord.
3. Roll the volume up smoothly over about one beat.
4. Roll it back down before the next note.

Curl your pinky around the volume knob while you pick, or use a volume/expression pedal (much easier).

### Make it a pad
- Long reverb (hall or shimmer), high mix.
- Optional: an octave-up effect or shimmer reverb for the "heavenly" sound.
- Play **slowly**: one swell per bar, or per chord.

### What to swell
- Single notes from the key (start with the 1 and the 5).
- Triads from ER.04, very slowly.
- Drone voicings from ER.06.

> Swell **in time**: start the swell on the beat so it peaks just after. Late swells sound like a mistake.

### Check yourself
Play a verse of a slow song with only swells. If it sounds like a pad (no clicks or pick attack), on two different days, mark it done.
