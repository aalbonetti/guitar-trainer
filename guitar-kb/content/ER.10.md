### Sixteenth-note groove
A muted sixteenth strum, locked with the drummer's hi-hat and kick, drives up-tempo songs and bridge builds.

{{rhythm:S8_sixteenths}}

### How
- The hand moves **down-up twice per beat**: 1 e & a, 2 e & a…
- Light palm mute or fret-hand mute so most strums are percussive "chks".
- Let **beats 2 and 4** pop out (lift the mute or strum harder), with the snare.
- Small motion from the wrist. Big arm motions can't keep up.

### Lock in
1. Muted strings only, 70 BPM, sixteenths. Count "1 e & a" out loud.
2. Add the accents on 2 and 4.
3. Play a chord (a triad or a barre) on the accents, muted chks in between.
4. Push to 90 BPM.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=80,beats=4,sub=4}}

> Listen for the **kick drum** in recordings: the groove sits on it. With a click only, imagine a drummer playing four on the floor.

### Goal
8 bars at 90 BPM, steady and even.
