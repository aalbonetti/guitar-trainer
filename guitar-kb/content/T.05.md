### The difference is one note
A major chord and a minor chord share the root and the 5th. Only the **3rd** changes: the major 3rd is one fret higher than the minor 3rd. That one fret is the difference between "bright" and "sad".

{{chords:E,Em,A,Am}}

### How to listen
- **Major** sounds settled, open, "home", bright.
- **Minor** sounds darker, more serious, a little unresolved.
- Sing the chord's notes from low to high. Major's middle note "lifts"; minor's middle note "sighs".

### Practice
1. Play E then Em, back and forth. Then A and Am. Notice which finger moves and what it does to the sound.
2. Take the quiz with your eyes closed. Use **Play again** as much as you like.

> If you're stuck, hum the chord's lowest note, then the middle one. Is the gap a small step (minor) or a bigger one (major)?
