### The worship guitarist's secret weapon
In the G family, your **ring and pinky never move**: they stay on fret 3 of strings 2 and 1. Only the index and middle fingers change. The changes get easy, and the two top notes (D and G) ring through every chord, which gives that shimmering, modern worship sound.

{{chords:G_4f,Cadd9,Dsus4,Em7}}

| Chord | Frets (6→1) | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| G | 3 2 0 0 3 3 | home |
| Cadd9 | x 3 2 0 3 3 | index and middle each move over one string |
| Dsus4 | x x 0 2 3 3 | index to string 3 fret 2 |
| Em7 | 0 2 2 0 3 3 | index and middle on fret 2 of strings 5 and 4 |

> Keep the pinky and ring pressed the whole time, even while the other fingers are in the air. That's the anchor.

### Put it in a song shape
The most common worship progression, 1–5–6m–4 in G, is just **G – Dsus4 – Em7 – Cadd9**. Two downstrums each, then four, then try the worship strum later in A.06.
