### The D family
These chords live in the **key of D** and the **key of A**, two of the most common worship keys without a capo.

{{chords:D,A,Asus4,A7sus4,Bm_partial}}

### Shapes
- **D** (`xx0232`): a small triangle on the top three strings. Strum only strings 4 to 1.
- **A** (`x02220`): three fingers in a row on fret 2. If they don't fit, use index, middle and ring squeezed tight, or one finger barring the three strings.
- **Asus4** (`x02230`): A with the 3rd raised to the 4th: string 2 moves from fret 2 up to fret 3. Finger it index-middle-ring (strings 4, 3, 2) so the ring finger can hop between fret 2 and 3. The A ↔ Asus4 move is a classic worship embellishment.
- **A7sus4** (`x02030`): an open, ringing A sound that works almost anywhere in the key of D.
- **Bm (partial)** (`xx4432`): the easy Bm without a full barre. Strum strings 4 to 1 only. The full barre comes in A.12.

### Changes to practice
- **D ↔ A**: keep the hand relaxed; the ring finger can pivot.
- **A ↔ Asus4**: just string 2 moving between fret 2 and fret 3. Use it to decorate a long A chord.
- **D ↔ Bm**: the biggest jump. Slow is fine.

> Muting the low strings matters here: strum from string 4 for D and Bm, from string 5 for A. Aim your strum, don't just swing at all six.

### Goal
25 clean changes a minute (D ↔ A, then D ↔ Bm).
