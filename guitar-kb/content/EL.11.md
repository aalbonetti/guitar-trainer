### Ambient lead
In quiet moments (intros, prayer times, tags), texture matters more than notes: bell-like harmonics and swelled melody notes over a long reverb.

### Natural harmonics
Touch the string **lightly directly above the fret wire** (don't press down), pick, then lift the finger.
- **Fret 12**: an octave above the open string. The easiest and loudest.
- **Fret 7**: an octave and a 5th up.
- **Fret 5**: two octaves up.

Across strings 3-2-1 at fret 12 you get G B E: a lovely Em(add) shimmer. At fret 7: D F# B, a soft Bm sound. Mix them over slow chords.

### Swelled melody
Play the melody notes from EL.07 with volume swells (ER.07) instead of picked attacks. Add a long reverb, maybe shimmer. The line floats.

### The graded run
{{exercise:EX_harmonics_swells}}

At your own pace: the nine harmonics on strings 3-2-1 (fret 12, then 7, then 5), then three notes swelled in with the volume knob (D, E on string 2, G on string 1). Let each one ring clearly before the next.

### Practice
1. Harmonics on frets 12, 7 and 5, every string. Get each one ringing clearly.
2. Over a slow G – C loop, play harmonics on the chord tones.
3. Swell a simple 3-note melody over the same loop.

{{tool:tuner}}

> Use the tuner to check: a clean 12th-fret harmonic on string 6 should read **E3**.

### Goal
Clean harmonics and swelled notes on the right pitches, 90% of the time.
