### Sitting
- Sit toward the front of the chair, back tall, shoulders loose.
- The guitar body rests on your right leg (for right-handed players), neck angled slightly up.
- Tilt the top of the guitar back only a little: you should *feel* the fretboard more than look at it.

### Fret hand
- Thumb on the back of the neck, roughly behind your middle finger. Not wrapped over the top for chords.
- Fingers arch like a bridge, pressing with the **tips**, so they don't touch the next string.
- Press **just behind the fret wire**, not in the middle of the space. You need far less pressure there.
- Keep your wrist fairly straight. If it bends sharply, move your elbow, not your wrist.

> Buzz? You're probably too far from the fret wire, or not pressing straight down. Dead note? A finger is leaning on the next string. Arch more.

### Holding the pick
- Lay the pick on the side of your index finger's first joint, point toward the strings.
- Pin it with the pad of your thumb. Only a few millimetres of tip should show.
- Grip just firmly enough that it doesn't spin. Loose wrist, loose forearm.
- For strumming, a thin or medium pick is more forgiving. For single notes, medium or heavy.

### Check yourself
Record 20 seconds of yourself on your phone, play any open string slowly, and watch it back: shoulders down? thumb behind the neck? fingertips arched? When all three look right on two different days, mark this lesson done.
