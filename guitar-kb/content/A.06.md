### Old faithful
This is the pattern you'll hear in most acoustic worship songs:

{{rhythm:S3_worship}}

Count it: **1**, **2** &, & **4** & → D, D U, U D U.

The hand still moves down-up on every eighth note. On the "&" of 1 and on beat 3 it just **misses** the strings (keeps moving, doesn't touch). That's the whole trick.

### Learn it in stages
1. Say it before you play it: "down, down-up, up-down-up".
2. Mute the strings with your fret hand and play the pattern on the dead strings at 60 BPM. Rhythm first, chords later.
3. One chord (G), 8 bars at 70.
4. Two chords per bar change: G for one bar, Cadd9 for one bar.
5. Real progression: **G – Dsus4 – Em7 – Cadd9**, one bar each, at 80, then 90.

{{tool:metronome:bpm=70,beats=4,sub=2}}

> The missed strum on beat 3 is what makes it feel like it "skips". If it sounds stiff, you're probably stopping your hand there instead of swinging past the strings.

### Goal
8 bars at 90 BPM, steady.
