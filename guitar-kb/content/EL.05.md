### Bends
Push the string sideways to raise its pitch. A bend is only good if it lands **exactly** in tune.

- **Whole-step bend**: raise the note 2 frets' worth. In the G box: string 3 fret 2 (A) bent up to sound like fret 4 (B). Or string 2 fret 3 (D) up to E.
- **Half-step bend**: 1 fret's worth. String 3 fret 4 (B) up to C.

### How
1. Play the **target note** first (fret 4 on string 3) and listen.
2. Fret the starting note (fret 2) with your **ring finger**, with the middle and index behind it on the same string for support.
3. Push the string toward the ceiling using your **wrist**, not just the finger, until it matches the target.
4. Use the **tuner**: bend and hold, and the needle shows how close you are.

### Vibrato
A gentle, regular wobble on a held note: bend slightly above pitch and back, about 5–6 times a second, driven by the wrist. Add it to the last note of every phrase.

> Under-bending (flat) is the most common mistake, and it sounds sour. Bend a little more than you think.

### Goal
Bends land within ±15 cents of the target pitch.
