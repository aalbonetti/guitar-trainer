### The parts that matter
You only need a handful of names to follow any lesson:

- **Headstock and tuning pegs**: where you tune.
- **Nut**: the slotted strip at the top of the neck. "Open" strings ring from here.
- **Frets**: the metal wires. "Fret 3" means the space *just behind* the third wire, counting from the nut.
- **Fretboard markers**: the dots at frets 3, 5, 7, 9 and the double dot at 12. Use them to find your place fast.
- **Bridge and saddle**: where the strings end. Picking near here sounds brighter, near the neck sounds warmer.
- **Electric only**: pickups, the volume and tone knobs, and the pickup selector.

### String numbers
Strings are numbered by **pitch, not position**. String **1** is the thinnest, highest string (closest to the floor when you hold the guitar). String **6** is the thickest, lowest.

| String | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note | E | A | D | G | B | E |

A way to remember low to high: **E**ddie **A**te **D**ynamite, **G**ood **B**ye **E**ddie.

> In every chord diagram and tab in this app, string 6 is on the left (diagrams) or at the bottom (tab). Same guitar, just drawn two ways.

### Standard tuning
Both E strings are E, two octaves apart. Every app lesson assumes standard tuning: **E A D G B E**. Next lesson you'll tune to it.
