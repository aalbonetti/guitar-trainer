### Octaves: the worship lead sound
Playing a melody as **octaves** (the same note on two strings at once) sounds big and clear, cuts through the band, and works with drive and delay. Most worship intro hooks are octaves.

### The shapes
| Low note on | High note on | Frets apart |
|---|---|---|
| String 6 | String 4 | +2 |
| String 5 | String 3 | +2 |
| String 4 | String 2 | +3 |
| String 3 | String 1 | +3 |

- Index on the low note, ring or pinky on the high note.
- The **string in between must be muted**: let the underside of your index finger touch it lightly.
- Strum through all three strings; only two should sound.

### Make a hook
Take three notes from the G major pentatonic, play them as octaves on strings 4 and 2, add the dotted-eighth delay (T.07) and a little drive. That's an intro.

### Goal
Correct notes 90% of the time, with the middle string silent.
