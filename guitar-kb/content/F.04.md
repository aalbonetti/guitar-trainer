### Chord diagrams
A chord diagram is the fretboard **standing up**, viewed from the front:

- Vertical lines are strings: **string 6 on the left**, string 1 on the right.
- Horizontal lines are frets. A thick top line is the nut. If you see a number beside the diagram instead, that's the starting fret.
- Dots show where to press; the number in the dot is the finger (1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky).
- **o** above a string = play it open. **×** = don't play it (or mute it).

### Tab
Tab is the fretboard **lying down**, as if you looked down at the neck while playing:

- Six lines, **string 1 on top**, string 6 on the bottom.
- Numbers are frets. `0` is an open string. Read left to right.
- Numbers stacked vertically are played together.

### Chord charts
Worship charts show chord names over the beat where the change happens. Many teams also use **numbers** instead of letters ("1 5 6m 4"); you'll learn those in T.03. In the Songs tab every chart can switch between chord names and numbers.
