### The C family
The key of C, and the chords you'll need when you capo up to play C-shape songs.

{{chords:C,Am,Fmaj7,G}}

### Shapes
- **C** (`x32010`): ring on string 5 fret 3, middle on string 4 fret 2, index on string 2 fret 1.
- **Am** (`x02210`): the same shape as E major, moved over one string. From C, only the ring finger moves (to string 3, fret 2).
- **Fmaj7** (`xx3210`): a friendly stand-in for F until barre chords. From C, the ring and middle fingers each move one string toward the floor (ring to string 4 fret 3, middle to string 3 fret 2); the index stays put.
- **G**: 3- or 4-finger, your choice.

### Try a progression
