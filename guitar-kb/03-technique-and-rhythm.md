## Fingerpicking patterns (p thumb, i index, m middle, a ring)

## Technique drills
**Catalog / app** is what the app plays or grades. **Extended practice** goes further on your own; it isn't graded.

| Drill | How | Catalog / app | Extended practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spider (1-2-3-4) | One finger per fret, frets 1–4, string 6 to string 1 | `EX_spider` (F.05): 39 → 60 BPM eighths, +4 BPM per step | Come back down, shift up a fret each cycle, push toward 120 BPM |
| One-minute changes | Switch between two chords as many times as you can in 60 s | `EX_changes_*`, goals 20–30/min by lesson. Open-chord pairs (`EX_changes_open`, 30/min): Em–Am, A–D, D–G, G–C, C–Am, E–Am, D–Dm, C–Fmaj7 | G–Em, C–F; push toward 60/min |
| Anchor/pivot practice | Find the fingers the two chords share and keep them down (G→Em, C→Am, D→Dsus2) | – | Smooth changes on the beat |
| Alternate picking | Strict D U on single notes: open strings, then the spider, then scales | Tempo ladder: +4 BPM after 3 clean passes | Open strings and scales |
| String crossing | Chromatic notes on string pairs; pick outside, then inside | – | 80 BPM sixteenths |
| Legato | Hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides, even volume | EL.03: 70 BPM eighths | Trills 5–7 and 5–8 on each string, 30 s |
| Slides | Slide 5→7 and back, legato and picked | Part of EL.03 | Land exactly on the target fret |
| Bends | G box: string 3 fret 2 up a whole step, string 2 fret 3 up a whole step, string 3 fret 4 up a half step | `EX_bends_G` (EL.05): within ±15 cents | Hold within ±10 cents; 1½-step bends; other frets |
| Vibrato | Wrist-driven, bending slightly above pitch and back | EL.05 lesson text | Even rate (~5–6 Hz), consistent width |
| Muting | Palm mute on the bridge; mute with the fret hand by releasing pressure | `S7_chug` (ER.02): 90 BPM | No unwanted ringing strings |
| Barre strength | Hold the F barre for 10 s, release, repeat. Check each string sounds | `EX_barre_F_Bm` (A.12) | Increase hold time weekly; avoid squeezing hard |
| Pentatonic sequences | Up and down the box, then groups of 3 and 4 | EL.01: 80 BPM eighths; Box Runner (up & down, threes) | Groups of 4; position 1 up to 140 BPM eighths |
| Finger independence | 1-3-2-4, 1-4-2-3, and other permutations | – | Clean at 80 BPM |

## Warm-up order (suggested)
1. Stretch hands and wrists (30 s)
2. Spider at a comfortable tempo (2 min)
3. Chord changes from the current lesson (3 min)
4. Strumming or picking pattern from the current lesson, with the metronome

## Common beginner fixes (for in-app tips)
- **Buzzing:** press just behind the fret wire, not in the middle of the fret.
- **Muted strings in a chord:** arch the fingers, keep the thumb lower on the back of the neck, and trim fingernails.
- **Strumming out of time:** keep the hand moving even on missed strums, and count out loud.
- **Barre doesn't sound:** roll the index finger slightly onto its bony edge, and pull back with the arm rather than squeezing with the thumb.
- **Pain:** a little fingertip soreness for the first 2–3 weeks is normal. Wrist or joint pain is not, so stop and check posture.
