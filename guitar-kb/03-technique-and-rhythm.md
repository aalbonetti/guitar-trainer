## Technique drills
| Drill | How | Target / progression |
|---|---|---|
| Spider (1-2-3-4) | One finger per fret, frets 1–4 on every string, up and back. Shift up one fret each cycle | 60 → 120 BPM eighths |
| One-minute changes | Switch between two chords as many times as you can in 60 s | 30 → 60/min. Pair order: Em–Am, A–D, D–G, G–C, C–Am, E–Am, D–Dm, G–Em, C–F(maj7) |
| Anchor/pivot practice | Find the fingers the two chords share and keep them down (G→Em, C→Am, D→Dsus2) | Smooth changes on the beat |
| Alternate picking | Strict D U on single notes: open strings, then the spider, then scales | Tempo ladder +4 BPM after 3 clean passes |
| String crossing | Chromatic notes on string pairs; pick outside, then inside | 80 BPM sixteenths |
| Legato | Hammer-on and pull-off trills: 5–7, 5–8 on each string, 30 s | Even volume between picked and slurred notes |
| Slides | Slide 5→7 and back, legato and picked | Land exactly on the target fret |
| Bends | Bend string 3 fret 7 up to the pitch of fret 9 (whole step) | App checks pitch within ±10 cents; also half-step and 1½-step bends |
| Vibrato | Wrist-driven, bending slightly above pitch and back | Even rate (~5–6 Hz), consistent width |
| Muting | Palm mute on the bridge; mute with the fret hand by releasing pressure | No unwanted ringing strings |
| Barre strength | Hold the F barre for 10 s, release, repeat. Check each string sounds | Increase hold time weekly; avoid squeezing hard |
| Pentatonic sequences | Groups of 3 and 4 up and down position 1 | 80 → 140 BPM eighths |
| Finger independence | 1-3-2-4, 1-4-2-3, and other permutations | Clean at 80 BPM |

## Warm-up order (suggested)
1. Stretch hands and wrists (30 s)
2. Spider at a comfortable tempo (2 min)
3. Chord changes from the current lesson (3 min)
4. Strumming or picking pattern from the current lesson, with the metronome

## Common beginner fixes (for in-app tips)
- **Buzzing:** press just behind the fret wire, not in the middle of the fret.
- **Muted strings in a chord:** arch the fingers, keep the thumb lower on the back of the neck, and trim fingernails.
- **Strumming out of time:** keep the hand moving even on missed strums, and count out loud.
- **Barre doesn't sound:** roll the index finger slightly onto its bony edge, and pull back with the arm rather than squeezing with the thumb.
- **Pain:** a little fingertip soreness for the first 2–3 weeks is normal. Wrist or joint pain is not, so stop and check posture.
