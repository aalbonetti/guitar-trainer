# Chord & Scale Reference

Frets are listed from string 6 → 1 (low E → high E). `x` = muted, `0` = open. Fingers: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky, T thumb.
The full chord library (828 chords, 3,283 voicings) comes from **chords-db** (MIT license, see the sources doc). The shapes below are the **curated beginner set** to use for lessons. Several of the database's first-listed voicings for these chords are non-standard or malformed, so don't take the database's default voicing blindly.

## Root-finding: note names
| Fret | 0 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| String 6 | E | F | G | A | B | C | D | E |
| String 5 | A | A#/Bb | C | D | E | F | G | A |
Natural notes have a whole step (2 frets) between them, except B–C and E–F, which are a half step (1 fret).

## A minor pentatonic — 5 positions (A C D E G)
Movable: shift every fret by the same amount to change key (G minor = −2 frets, so position 1 starts at fret 3).
| Pos | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 8 | 5 7 | 5 7 | 5 7 | 5 8 | 5 8 |
| 2 | 8 10 | 7 10 | 7 10 | 7 9 | 8 10 | 8 10 |
| 3 | 10 12 | 10 12 | 10 12 | 9 12 | 10 13 | 10 12 |
| 4 | 12 15 | 12 15 | 12 14 | 12 14 | 13 15 | 12 15 |
| 5 | 15 17 | 15 17 | 14 17 | 14 17 | 15 17 | 15 17 |
Position 5 is the same pattern as the open-position shape an octave up (subtract 12: 3 5 | 3 5 | 2 5 | 2 5 | 3 5 | 3 5).
- **C major pentatonic** (the relative major) uses the same five boxes. Only the root changes, to C.
- **A blues scale** = minor pentatonic + b5 (Eb). In position 1, add fret 6 on string 5 and fret 8 on string 3.

## G major scale — one position (root on string 6, fret 3)
| String | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frets | 2 3 5 | 2 3 5 | 2 4 5 | 2 4 5 | 3 5 | 2 3 5 |
The pattern is movable. To change key, put the root (the middle finger on string 6) on the new key's fret: A = 5, C = 8, D = 10.
